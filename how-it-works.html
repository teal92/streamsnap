How it works

Creator-controlled from source to publish.

StreamSnap HQ assists with discovery, editing and publishing, while the creator remains in control of connected accounts and the media that is sent to them.

1

Connect authorized sources and accounts

The operator chooses livestream or video sources they are permitted to process and connects destination accounts through each platform’s official authorization flow. StreamSnap HQ does not ask for platform passwords.

2

Detect and prepare candidate moments

The desktop application can analyze activity, speech and timing signals to identify possible short-form moments. Candidate clips can be formatted vertically and prepared with subtitles and draft metadata.

3

Review the clip

The operator can preview the rendered media and review or edit titles, captions and destination settings before publication. Automated output may be changed or rejected.

4

Publish through official APIs

Approved media is sent only to accounts explicitly connected by the operator. Platform privacy and publishing options are limited to the choices that the destination platform makes available to that account and application.

5

Track status and disconnect at any time

StreamSnap HQ tracks upload status and permitted account metrics for the connected workflow. Authorization can be revoked from the destination platform or removed from StreamSnap HQ.

StreamSnap HQ is a desktop creator tool. Platform availability, permissions and publishing visibility are controlled by each connected service.
