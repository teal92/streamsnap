Desktop creator workflow

Turn livestreams and videos into short-form clips.

StreamSnap HQ is desktop software that helps a creator identify notable moments, prepare vertical short-form clips, review titles and captions, and publish approved media to social accounts the creator explicitly connects.

Monitor

Analyze authorized public livestream sources for activity and potential moments.

Review

Prepare clips for human review, including formatting, captions, titles, and descriptions.

Publish

Send only operator-approved clips to connected platforms through their official APIs.

StreamSnap HQ does not ask for social-account passwords. Connections use each platform’s authorization process, and publishing access can be revoked from the platform account.
