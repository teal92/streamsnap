Privacy Policy

Effective September 12, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how StreamSnap handles information when an operator connects social accounts, processes livestream media, reviews clips, and publishes approved content. StreamSnap is designed primarily as locally operated software.

2. Information StreamSnap processes

  • Connected-account information: platform-issued access and refresh tokens, account or channel identifiers, usernames, Page information, and granted permissions.
  • Media and publishing information: source recordings, selected clips, rendered video, subtitles, titles, descriptions, destinations, upload identifiers, and publication status.
  • Operational information: application settings, processing diagnostics, errors, capture-health results, queue state, and aggregate publishing metrics.

3. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate connected accounts, identify and prepare potential clips, show clips for operator review, publish operator-approved media, track publication status, diagnose failures, prevent duplicate work, and maintain aggregate operational metrics.

4. Platform authorization

StreamSnap uses OAuth or equivalent authorization provided by each connected platform. StreamSnap does not request or store the operator’s TikTok, Meta, or Google password. Platform-issued tokens are used only for the permissions shown during authorization and required for the requested publishing workflow.

5. Storage and retention

Application records, credentials, media, and diagnostics are stored on the computer where the operator runs StreamSnap unless information must be transmitted to a connected platform to perform an authorized action.

  • Rejected or failed intermediate media may be deleted after diagnostics are retained.
  • When a final clip reaches the review queue, unneeded source recordings, intermediate renders, metadata caches, and temporary files may be removed.
  • A queued production clip may be retained until every selected destination is published or the operator deletes it.
  • After successful publication to every selected destination, the final media file may be deleted while aggregate publishing metrics and necessary status records remain.
  • Authorization tokens remain until they expire, are replaced, are revoked, or the connection is removed.

6. Sharing

StreamSnap does not sell personal information. Information is transmitted to TikTok, Meta, YouTube, or another connected service only as needed to authenticate the operator, retrieve permitted account details, upload approved media, or check publication status. Those platforms process information under their own privacy policies.

7. Website data

This informational website does not intentionally use accounts, forms, advertising trackers, analytics, or cookies. The hosting provider may process basic request information such as IP address, browser type, and access time for security and delivery under its own policies.

8. Security

Reasonable safeguards are used to limit access to stored credentials and media. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Operators should secure the device running StreamSnap, protect configuration files, and revoke connected-platform access if compromise is suspected.

9. Your choices and deletion

The operator can stop future access by disconnecting an account in StreamSnap or revoking access from the connected platform’s security settings. Locally stored media, logs, and application data can be deleted by the operator. For assistance with data associated with StreamSnap, use the contact address below.

10. Children

StreamSnap is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through this website.

11. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when StreamSnap’s processing or platform integrations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

12. Contact

Privacy questions or deletion requests can be sent to streamsnaphq@gmail.com.
